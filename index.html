Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our retainer and billing process. Can't find the answer you're looking for? Reach out to us directly at hello@nova.studio.

How does the "pause" feature work?

If you have a slow week or don't have enough requests to fill a full month, you can pause your subscription. Our billing cycles are based on a 31-day period, meaning any unused days are saved and can be reactivated whenever you need.

What is the typical turnaround time?

Most design requests (landing page sections, app screen updates, UI component designs) are delivered in under 48 hours. More complex features or design system rebuilds are broken down into logical daily milestones.

Do you offer development services?

Yes! We offer high-fidelity Webflow and Framer development as part of our package. Custom coding (React/Tailwind) is available upon request.

How do we communicate?

We set up a private Slack channel for day-to-day asynchronous updates and use a shared Notion board to organize design tickets. We also offer optional weekly sync calls.
